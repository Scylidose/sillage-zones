# Attribution

The boundary data in this repository is derived from:

- **OpenStreetMap** — hydrography (water masks), parks, and restricted areas
  (airports, military zones, prisons), queried through the Overpass API.
  © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the
  [Open Database License](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/) (ODbL).
- **Données ouvertes de la Ville de Montréal** — arrondissement boundaries.
- **Données Québec / Statistics Canada** — municipal boundaries.

Two files describe rather than delimit, and come from Wikimedia:

- `zone-info.json` — each zone's item, names, population and area from
  **Wikidata**, available under [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
- `curiosities.json` — titles and coordinates of geolocated **Wikipedia**
  articles (French and English), whose text is available under
  [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/); each entry
  links back to its article.

Because these packs are a derived database of ODbL material, they are
distributed under the **ODbL** as well: you may copy, modify and redistribute
them, provided you attribute OpenStreetMap and keep any redistributed
derivative under the same licence.
